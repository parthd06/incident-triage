# Policy-Controlled Incident Runbook

Incident ID: inc-001

Incident: checkout-api 5xx rate above 15%: checkout-api is returning HTTP 500 on POST /checkout for about 1 in 6 requests since 14:12 UTC. Customers cannot complete orders.

Severity: SEV1

Summary: checkout-api is failing ~18% of POST /checkout requests with HTTP 500 errors due to Hikari connection pool exhaustion connecting to orders-db following deploy d-1042.

Likely cause: Deploy d-1042 reduced checkout-api's database maximumPoolSize from 50 to 5, starving worker threads and triggering 3000ms connection acquisition timeouts (SQLTransientConnectionException).

Rationale: Logs and metrics show connections dropped from 41 to 5 at 14:02 UTC after deploy d-1042, causing pool wait queues to surge to 37 and p95 latency to jump over 3000ms with corresponding 500 responses.

Findings:
- logs (SEV1): checkout-api is throwing HTTP 500 on POST /checkout due to database connection pool exhaustion (SQLTransientConnectionException) starting at 2026-10-09T14:12:03Z.
- deploys (SEV1): Deploy d-1042 to checkout-api at 14:02 UTC drastically reduced the database connection pool size, leading to connection exhaustion and a >15% HTTP 500 failure rate on POST /checkout.
- metrics (SEV1): checkout-api experienced a sharp rise in error rates and latency starting around 14:10 UTC following a connection pool / database connection drop at 14:05 UTC. By 14:15 UTC, checkout-api error rate reached 17.8% (peaking at 18.4% at 14:20 UTC) and p95 latency increased from 230ms to over 3000ms due to database connection pool exhaustion.

Approved actions:
- payments / checkout-api: Roll back deploy d-1042 to restore Hikari maximumPoolSize from 5 to 50. (approved by team)

Audit log:
- Router chose ['logs', 'deploys', 'metrics']: Critical failure on core checkout flow with a 15% 5xx error rate directly blocking customer transactions and causing active revenue loss.
- Model combined 3 findings into one analysis.
- Policy check: 2 of 2 actions need team approval.
- Team payments decision for checkout-api: approve.
- Team platform-data decision for orders-db: reject.
- Published runbook to runbooks/inc-001.md.
